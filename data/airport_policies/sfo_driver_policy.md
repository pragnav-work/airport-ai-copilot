# SFO Driver Policy

## Queue Rules

Drivers must enter and remain in the designated airport queue when waiting for airport requests.

Drivers should not leave the queue after joining unless they have a valid operational reason.

## Cancellation

Repeated driver cancellations may trigger an operational review.

A high driver cancellation rate should be investigated when airport completion rate declines.

## Incentives

Driver incentives may be considered when available supply is insufficient to meet airport demand.

Incentive decisions should consider driver shortage severity and expected operational impact.
