# LAX Operations Policy

## Completion Rate

The expected airport completion rate is above 84%.

If completion rate falls below 84%, Operations should investigate queue size, driver availability, ETA, and cancellation rate.

## Pickup and Drop-off

Drivers must use designated airport pickup and drop-off areas.

Drivers must follow airport traffic management instructions.

## Queue

Drivers waiting for airport requests must use the designated staging queue.

Drivers should not bypass other drivers in the queue.

## Late-Night Operations

Late-night operations are defined as 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM.
