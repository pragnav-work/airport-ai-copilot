# JFK Operations Policy

## Completion Rate

The expected airport completion rate is above 86%.

A completion rate below 86% should trigger operational investigation.

## Pickup

Drivers must use designated pickup zones.

Drivers must follow airport traffic and staging requirements.

## Queue

Drivers must remain in the designated airport queue while waiting for airport requests.

Queue jumping is prohibited.

## Late-Night Operations

Late-night operations are defined as 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM.
