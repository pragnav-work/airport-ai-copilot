# LAX Driver Policy

## Queue

Drivers must use the designated airport staging queue.

Drivers must not intentionally bypass the queue.

## Cancellation

High driver cancellation rates should be investigated when completion rate falls below the operational threshold.

Repeated cancellations may result in an operational review.

## Incentives

Driver incentives may be offered when supply is insufficient to meet demand.

The incentive should be based on the severity of the supply shortage.
