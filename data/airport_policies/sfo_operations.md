# SFO Operations Policy

## Completion Rate

The expected airport completion rate is above 85%.

If completion rate falls below 85%, Operations should investigate driver availability, queue conditions, cancellation rate, and ETA.

## Queue

Drivers must remain in the designated airport queue while waiting for pickup requests.

Drivers must not abandon the queue solely because demand is temporarily low.

## Pickup

Drivers may collect passengers only from designated pickup areas.

Drivers must follow airport traffic and staging instructions.

## Late-Night Operations

Late-night airport operations are defined as 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM.

Additional monitoring is recommended during late-night periods because driver availability may be lower.
